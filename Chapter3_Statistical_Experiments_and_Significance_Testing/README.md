## Key takeaway

"Statistically significant" is not the same as "real" or "important" — a
p-value only tells you how surprising your result would be under the
assumption of no effect. Every significance test in this chapter
(permutation, t-test, ANOVA, chi-square) needs to be paired with adequate
sample size (power analysis) to be trustworthy; an under-powered "null
result" often just means the study couldn't have detected the effect even
if it were real.
